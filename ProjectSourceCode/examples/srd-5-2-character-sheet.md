# SRD 5.2 Character Sheet

The builder changes needed for this sheet are implemented:

- **Groups** arrange related fields in compact, configurable panels; **Pages** provide tabbed navigation.
- Formula modules support ability modifiers, proficiency bonus, minimum/maximum, and numeric lookups. Choice fields can select linked values, and formulas can be hidden from Playtest.
- The sheet includes a selectable spellcasting ability with derived attack bonus and save DC, plus prepared/equipped checkboxes and rest/location choices in its tables.
- Repeatable tables support checkbox and choice cells, plus per-row links to calculated values.

Portraits and persistence of player-entered values are intentionally deferred until character support is implemented. The template uses labels and fields only, not SRD rules text. SRD 5.2.1 is licensed under CC BY 4.0, not public domain.
